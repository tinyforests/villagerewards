# Village Rewards: AI context

Village Rewards is an independent local loyalty product. Its purpose is to make shopping at participating village traders easy, enjoyable and rewarding.

Read `AGENTS.md` for current engineering facts and `docs/BRAND.md` for design rules. The visual reference is `docs/brand-book.html`; shared CSS tokens are in `assets/brand.css`.

## Voice and appearance

Sunny, playful and easy. Write short, useful sentences. Explain the next action, the points cost and what the customer receives. Celebrate real achievements. Use white, butter yellow, pink and mint, with charcoal text. Use Nunito Sans and rounded, generous controls.

## Product integrity

- Label demo offers and mock codes explicitly.
- Never imply unverified merchant participation, rewards, availability or commercial results.
- State redemption conditions before confirmation.
- Keep primary actions visible and put secondary customer destinations behind a labelled Menu button.
- Do not change earning, authentication or redemption rules during a brand-only task.
- Treat SQL and browser source as implementation evidence, not proof of deployed configuration.
- Public contact email is coming soon; do not activate its link until the mailbox is connected.

## Practical review

Can the customer read it, understand it and complete the next action? Does it feel happy and welcoming? Are all claims true for the current pilot? Does it work with a keyboard, enlarged text and reduced motion?
