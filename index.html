Good to know

Little questions. Clear answers.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Village Rewards works in your phone’s browser. Open the app and register to get your village code.

How do points work?

In the current pilot, $1 spent earns 1 point, with purchase points rounded to the nearest whole point. Staff confirm your purchase at participating shops.

Are the rewards live?

The current customer experience includes a clearly labelled demonstration of reward tiers. Demo reward codes cannot be used in shops. Check the app and pilot team for live availability.

What happens when I redeem a tier reward?

The current tier system resets your village points to zero when a merchant confirms a live tier redemption. Shop stamp rewards work separately. Always read the confirmation before claiming. Demo claims do not change your balance.

I’m a trader. How do I start?

Speak with the Village Rewards pilot team about joining the local group. The trader presentation explains the pilot. Existing traders can access their login from the app.
