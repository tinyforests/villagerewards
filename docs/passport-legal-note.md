# Village Passport — legal note (trade promotion lottery)

> Draft note for the Village Passport prize draw. **Not legal advice — confirm with
> the VGCCC and/or a lawyer before the first public promotion.**

## What this is

The Village Passport runs a periodic prize draw: customers who visit a required number
of different participating traders (with a qualifying spend) during a campaign period go
into a draw for a prize (launch = iPhone; later rounds = trader voucher hamper). In
Victoria this is a **trade promotion lottery**, regulated by the **VGCCC** (Victorian
Gambling and Casino Control Commission).

## Permit

- A trade promotion lottery permit is **often not required** when entry depends only on
  a **purchase and visit** with **no separate entry fee** (the prize is a "free" draw
  incidental to ordinary trading).
- This exemption has conditions and thresholds. **Confirm current VGCCC guidance before
  launch** — do not rely on this note. If any paid-for-entry element is introduced, a
  permit is likely required.

## Required regardless of permit

- **Written Terms & Conditions**, published and available to entrants, covering at least:
  eligibility, campaign period (start/end), how to enter (visit N traders, $10+ per
  visit, one stamp per trader per day), prize description and value, draw date/method,
  how winners are notified and results published, and the promoter's details.
- **Record-keeping** of entries and the draw (who qualified, when, the draw outcome),
  retained per VGCCC requirements.
- Clear identification of the **promoter / funder** (e.g. the village association or
  council) on all promotional material.

## Before first public promotion — checklist

- [ ] Confirm permit position with VGCCC for the specific prize value and entry mechanics.
- [ ] Draft and publish written T&Cs.
- [ ] Define the draw method and record-keeping process.
- [ ] Confirm who the legal promoter is and that they accept the obligations.
- [ ] Re-check for each subsequent round (prize value/mechanics may change the position).
