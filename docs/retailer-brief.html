How it works

Customers open Village Rewards in their phone browser and create an account. At a participating business, they make a qualifying purchase and show their customer code to staff.

Staff confirm the purchase and award one village stamp, regardless of the amount spent. Customers collect stamps across participating businesses in one shared balance, then exchange them for available rewards in the village marketplace.

Redeeming uses the listed number of stamps; customers keep the rest. Scanning the counter QR opens the service. It does not award a stamp on its own.

You choose whether to contribute a reward

You can participate by awarding stamps without supplying a marketplace reward. There is no compulsory reward contribution.

The marketplace is supplied by retailers who volunteer an offer. If you contribute, you choose the reward, its stamp cost, quantity and conditions. Customers may redeem stamps earned at other participating shops against your listed offer. You cover the cost of the reward you provide.

A coffee, a service or a discount could be an offer. These are examples; the actual catalogue will be confirmed before launch.

Your own promotions are optional

You may also choose to run a separate loyalty offer for your own customers, such as buy 10 coffees and get one free. Your shop card follows your own qualifying-purchase rules and stays separate from the shared village balance.

Enhanced retailer tools may be offered separately in future. Any paid features will be optional, demonstrated and clearly priced before you choose them.

What retailers can access

  • A business listing so village shoppers can find you and see your offers.
  • Participation in shared village rewards and discovery campaigns when available.
  • Potential optional extras: enhanced digital loyalty cards and promotion tools that you control.
  • Potential optional extras: enhanced reporting on recorded visits, repeat use and redemptions.
  • Counter QR materials, onboarding and support.

Free participation and the pilot

The founding pilot runs for 90 days. Basic participation is free during and after the pilot, with no automatic subscription, transaction fees or percentage of sales.

We are seeking association or sponsor support to operate the shared service; funding is still to be agreed. Any reward you choose to offer remains your own promotional cost. Future enhanced tools may be offered at an optional price, with clear terms before you opt in.

The pilot will test customer use, cross-shopping and retailer value. Participation does not guarantee extra sales.

Helping customers discover your business

A proposed launch challenge invites customers to purchase at six different participating businesses to enter a prize draw. MAVBA or sponsors may support the prize alongside operating funding. The campaign will only be announced once funding, dates and terms are agreed.

Customers do not need to visit six businesses to use ordinary marketplace rewards. The challenge is a separate incentive to explore.

What we need from participating retailers

  • Confirm your business details and nominate someone to manage participation.
  • Give staff access to a suitable internet-connected phone, tablet or computer and practise the purchase-confirmation process.
  • Display your counter QR code, explain the program to interested customers and give feedback during the pilot.
  • If you volunteer a reward, keep availability accurate and honour accepted claims under the agreed terms.
