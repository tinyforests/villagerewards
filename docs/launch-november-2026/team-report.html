01 / One shared understanding

We are preparing a 90-day founding pilot for November 2026. The exact launch date remains to be agreed. The latest model uses one village stamp per qualifying purchase and a voluntary retailer reward marketplace.

This report finalises the planning brief, not the product release. The existing app still contains older points rules. Launch depends on engineering verification, a real reward catalogue, trained traders and agreed operating terms.
AreaNovember planning baseline
EarningOne village stamp per qualifying purchase, regardless of spend. The earlier 1/3/5 spending brackets are superseded.
MarketplaceOne shared village balance. Rewards supplied only by retailers who volunteer. Participation does not require a reward contribution.
RedemptionSpend the listed stamp cost and retain the remaining balance. Rewards starting at 10 stamps remain the catalogue target; actual offers and stock must be secured.

Pilot and priceFree basic retailer participation, including listing, awarding village stamps and participation in available village campaigns. The 90-day pilot is a review period, not a billing trigger. Any future paid tools are optional, with pricing and terms agreed before opt-in.
Discovery campaignProposed purchase-at-six-different-businesses draw. One entry per qualifying customer is recommended, pending approved terms. No first-to-100 race.
MAVBASeek endorsement, business introductions and association or sponsor support for operating the shared service and any prize campaign. Funding is not yet committed. Everyday marketplace rewards remain voluntary retailer contributions.

04 / The counter experience

  1. The customer opens Village Rewards from the counter QR or saved browser link and accesses their account.
  2. The customer makes a qualifying purchase and shows their customer code.
  3. Authorised staff identify the customer and confirm the purchase. The system awards one village stamp.
  4. If the retailer runs an optional shop card, eligible items count under that card’s separate conditions.
  5. The customer browses available marketplace rewards. At collection, the contributing business validates the claim once.

A QR scan alone is not purchase confirmation. Staff need a tested manual fallback if the camera fails. Marketplace rewards can be earned elsewhere; reward contributors must understand and accept that before listing an offer.

DecisionWhy it matters
Qualifying purchase and repeat-purchase policyDefine exclusions, split transactions, same-day repeat purchases and corrections. The one-stamp promise must match enforcement.
Reward availabilityConfirm actual contributors and enough accessible rewards. Voluntary supply does not guarantee stock; nominate who replenishes and handles shortages.
Existing pointsApprove treatment of genuine pilot balances and demo activity; communicate conversion before changing value.
Refunds, expiry and fulfilmentCustomers and staff need the same rules for reversals, claim expiry, unavailable items and retained stamps.
Operating funding and optional toolsAgree the operating budget, funding owner and association or sponsor support. Define free basic access. Price enhanced tools only after demand validation and require explicit opt-in.
Prize campaignConfirm prize, sponsor, dates, eligibility, one-entry rule, selection process and applicable promotional/privacy requirements with the responsible reviewer.
Support and data accessActivate a support channel; agree data access, retention and escalation. Individual customer movement is not a shared marketing list.

06 / Launch decision

Ready to earn

Identity, staff permissions and one-stamp awards verified. Duplicates and refunds handled. Staff trained.

Ready to reward

Real offers, stock, clear conditions and a working claim-to-collection journey. No mock offers presented as available.

Ready to support

Named owners, working contact channel, approved terms, monitoring and a recovery plan.

If the prize is not ready: launch the core program only if its own gates pass, and leave the draw unadvertised. If earning or redemption is not reliable, or rewards are unavailable: delay public enrolment rather than build an unusable balance.

07 / Pilot scorecard

Review activated businesses, customers making a first purchase, repeat participation, customers shopping at multiple businesses, fulfilled rewards, available stock, support issues and trader continuation intent. Set targets before launch; no numeric success threshold has been agreed yet.

The central question: Are customers finding useful rewards and trying more local businesses, while traders find the service worth continuing? Recorded purchases show use, not proof of additional sales.
