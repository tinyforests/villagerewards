The agreed direction

AreaNovember planning baseline
EarningOne village stamp per qualifying purchase, regardless of spend. The earlier 1/3/5 spending brackets are superseded.
MarketplaceOne shared village balance. Rewards supplied only by retailers who volunteer. Participation does not require a reward contribution.
RedemptionSpend the listed stamp cost and retain the remaining balance. Rewards starting at 10 stamps remain the catalogue target; actual offers and stock must be secured.
Shop promotionsOptional, separate retailer loyalty cards and offers. A shop card measures qualifying purchases at that shop; village stamps do not prove loyalty to one retailer.
Pilot and price90 days free for founding businesses; proposed ongoing price of A$30 per month per business, with no transaction fee or percentage of sales. Billing start, GST treatment and cancellation terms need confirmation.
Discovery campaignProposed purchase-at-six-different-businesses draw. One entry per qualifying customer is recommended, pending approved terms. No first-to-100 race.
MAVBARequested endorsement, business introductions and funding or sourcing of a launch prize. No association funding for everyday marketplace rewards. Support is not yet evidenced as committed.

How to use this pack

Read the team brief first for a shared understanding. Engineering owns verification of the implementation; marketing uses only features and offers confirmed ready. The November target is conditional on the documented launch gates.

Each report supports mobile reading and browser printing to PDF. Visual styling follows the repository brand book. These are shareable planning documents; hosting them does not imply MAVBA endorsement or a confirmed prize.
