01 / Positioning

Shop local. Score more.

Village Rewards is a shared local loyalty program. Customers collect a village stamp when they make a qualifying purchase at a participating business, then choose from available rewards contributed by local retailers.

Participation and contribution are different. A retailer can award village stamps without supplying a marketplace reward. Contributing retailers choose what to offer and how much they can provide.

02 / Explain it in four steps

1. JoinOpen Village Rewards in your phone browser.
2. ShopMake a qualifying purchase and show your customer code to staff.
3. CollectStaff confirm the purchase. One village stamp is added.
4. ChooseSave towards an available reward and follow its collection instructions.

A counter QR opens the service; it does not verify a purchase or award a stamp on its own. No dedicated hardware is intended, but staff need access to a suitable device and the trader workflow.

06 / Claims and FAQs

Question or claimAccurate answer
Does spending more earn more stamps?No. This version rewards qualifying purchases, regardless of amount. Do not claim proportional recognition for large spending.
Must I shop at six businesses to redeem?No. Ordinary marketplace rewards use your village stamp balance. The proposed six-shop rule applies to the separate prize draw.
Does every shop give a reward?No. Marketplace contributions are voluntary. Check the available catalogue.
Can I use stamps like cash anywhere?No. Redeem against listed offers and conditions; there is no universal cash value promised.
Does redeeming erase all my stamps?Launch design: deduct the reward cost and keep the rest. Existing prototype reset behaviour must be replaced before using this answer publicly.
Is it an installed app?It is accessed through a mobile browser. Do not promise verified standalone installation.
Will the program increase my sales?It is designed to encourage discovery and return visits. The pilot will measure use; sales uplift is not guaranteed.
Who funds rewards?Volunteering retailers fund their offers. MAVBA is being asked to support the separate campaign prize, not the everyday reward pool.

07 / November launch work

  1. Before announcement: confirm founding traders, usable rewards, offer quantities, collection details, support and approved pricing terms.
  2. Before printing: align all point-based app, website and decal copy with the new stamp model. Proof QR destinations on physical phones.
  3. Launch week: demonstrate one complete purchase-to-reward journey; give staff a short script and customers a clear catalogue.
  4. Following weeks: feature consenting businesses and actual available offers. Pause promotion of exhausted rewards immediately.

Use the brand book’s white canvas, butter yellow, mint and pink accents, charcoal text and Nunito Sans. Use real local imagery with permission. Display reward price and conditions together. The planned hello@villagerewards.com.au mailbox is not verified active; confirm a support channel before launch.

08 / What to measure

  • Signup-to-first-confirmed-purchase rate, with a defined observation window.
  • Repeat purchase activity within 30 days, using only customers with a full observation window.
  • Percentage of purchasing customers with purchases at two or more distinct shops; show the denominator and campaign period.
  • Reward claim and fulfilment rates, stock availability and complaints.
  • Founding trader activation, weekly activity and demand for optional enhanced tools. Test willingness to pay before setting prices. Separate recorded spending from sales caused by the program.
