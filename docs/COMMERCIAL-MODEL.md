# Village Rewards commercial direction

Updated 26 October 2026. Supersedes the earlier A$30 monthly retailer subscription proposal.

- Basic retailer participation is free: business listing, awarding village stamps and participation in available village campaigns.
- The 90-day pilot is for evaluation, not an automatic billing trigger.
- Seek association or sponsor funding for the shared service. An operating budget, accountable owner and funding commitment are still needed.
- Enhanced retailer loyalty, promotion and reporting tools may become optional paid extras. No price or package is agreed. Demonstrate value and obtain explicit opt-in before charging.
- Marketplace reward contributions remain voluntary and funded by contributing retailers.
- Prize campaign funding is separate and unconfirmed. MAVBA support must not be described as secured.
- Do not build automatic post-pilot billing or gate basic participation behind a paid subscription.

This is a commercial decision, not verification of deployed functionality or sustainable funding.
