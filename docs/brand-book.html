01 / The feeling

Make everyday rewarding.

Village Rewards turns familiar local routines into things to look forward to. The brand should feel welcoming on a shop window, easy at the counter and happy in your pocket.

Sunny

Warm colour, optimistic words and small celebrations that make progress feel good.

Playful

Round shapes, chunky numbers, reward stickers and a little surprise in the composition.

Easy

Readable text, clear points prices and one obvious next action. People should know what to do immediately.

Main line: Shop local. Score more.
Supporting line: Your everyday favourites. Your next little treat.

Soft charcoal · #29332F
All essential text, icons and primary buttons. White text on charcoal buttons.

Starting balance: roughly 60% white, 30% pastel surfaces and 10% charcoal details. Use one dominant pastel per card and usually two per screen. These proportions are a composition guide.

Use charcoal text on every pastel. Reserve pastels for backgrounds, not small lettering. Always pair status colour with words or an icon. Purple is outside this first palette.

03 / Type & mark

Round, bold and readable.

Use Nunito Sans throughout: 900 for short headlines and points, 800 for buttons, 400–600 for body text. Its soft letterforms carry the friendliness while one family keeps everything consistent.

App sizes: 16px body, 14px supporting text, 24–32px headings, 48–64px points. Keep controls at least 48px tall. Let text wrap and grow when users increase their text size.

Working wordmark: lowercase, heavy and friendly. A small four-point sparkle marks a little win. Keep clear space around the mark at least the height of its “v”; use charcoal on white or a pastel. This is a direction for a future logo master.

Illustrative interface specimens. Offers and numbers are examples; buttons on this page are visual samples. Emoji stands in for commissioned illustrations.

  • Shapes: 24px card corners, 16px button corners and round reward badges.
  • Illustration: simple coffee cups, pastries, shopping bags and gifts, with charcoal outlines and pastel fills. Keep the same drawing style throughout.
  • Photography: real treats and local shopkeepers, daylight, close crops and uncluttered backgrounds.
  • Motion: a brief sparkle or point count-up when points arrive. Aim for 200–400ms feedback; honour reduced-motion settings and show the result immediately.
  • Navigation: labelled ☰ Menu in the header. Keep Show my code and Browse rewards visible on Home.
05 / Sound like a friendly local

A little cheek. Lots of clarity.

Use short, warm sentences. Put the useful information first. Save the celebration for good news and make problems easy to resolve.

WELCOMEHey Alex. Good things ahead.
PROGRESS3 more points. One lovely coffee.
POINTS EARNEDNice one. You earned 8 points.
DISCOVERYYour next favourite is around the corner.
EMPTY STATEYour first treat starts with your first shop.
ERRORWe couldn’t load your points. Please try again.

Use “free” only when it is accurate and show any conditions nearby. Keep merchant names, points costs and availability plain. Avoid invented urgency, guilt about missed visits and unexplained loyalty jargon.

Made for your local printer.

Each SVG is set to 200 × 200 mm with lettering converted to vector outlines. The background shape is the finished edge; the surrounding space in the preview is not part of the decal.

Suggested finish: removable window vinyl with an opaque white backing to keep the pastels bright. For fitting inside the window and reading from outside, ask for a reverse-printed, face-adhesive version.

These are finished-size artwork masters. Your printer should add their required bleed and cut contour, convert colours for their print process, and supply a colour and adhesion proof before the full run. Keep the website legible if resizing.
