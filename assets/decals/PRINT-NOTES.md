## Artwork

SVG vector artwork at finished size. Nunito Sans 900 lettering is converted to paths; no font installation or external assets required. Font source: Google Fonts, Nunito Sans, SIL Open Font License. All artwork scales as vectors. Preserve proportions and check the website lettering at the proposed physical size.

The outer background shape defines the intended finished edge. No bleed, crop marks or machine-specific cut contour is included. Printer to add bleed beyond that edge and a separate non-printing cut contour to their own specification. Do not print a rectangular white surround on the circular designs.

## Production brief

Suggested material: removable window vinyl with an opaque white backing, subject to the printer confirming suitability for the shop glass and intended exposure. Confirm installation side before production:

- Outside application: normal-reading artwork and rear adhesive.
- Inside application, viewed from outside: printer to prepare reversed artwork, face adhesive and appropriate white backing. Files supplied here are normal-reading.

Colours are supplied as RGB brand references. Printer to convert using their equipment/material profile and provide a physical colour proof: pastel saturation and opacity depend on material, ink and backing. Confirm size, cut, adhesion, removability and UV durability on a sample before the full run. No material or lifespan guarantee is implied by the artwork.

These decals identify participating shops; confirm shop participation before installation. They include the public website address, with no QR or NFC tracking embedded.
