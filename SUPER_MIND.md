# Village Rewards product context

This file remains as a compatibility entry point for older repository references.

Village Rewards has its own independent identity and product direction. Current instructions are in:

- `AI_CONTEXT.md`: voice, principles and product integrity.
- `AGENTS.md`: engineering state and constraints.
- `docs/BRAND.md`: palette, typography, components and navigation.
- `docs/brand-book.html`: visual brand book.

The focus is local shops, shared loyalty and simple, happy reward experiences. Keep consumer copy concrete and keep the customer’s next action easy to find.
